The answer proposes a zone block with forme "cone", longueur 3, anchored on the caster (origine "lanceur"), with an orientation, and with decalage 1. It explains why decalage is 1: the offset counts from the anchor cell inclusive, so with 0 the first step of the cone would be the caster's own cell.
