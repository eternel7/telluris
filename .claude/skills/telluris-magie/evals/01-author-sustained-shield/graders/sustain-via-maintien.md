The answer models the "stays up while the caster pays each round" behaviour with a per-round PM upkeep field (`maintien`), kept separate from the one-off launch cost (`cout_pm`). It does NOT model it as a duration that counts down each turn. It says (or the doc makes clear) that the upkeep costs no action points, and that the shield ends when the caster can no longer pay.
