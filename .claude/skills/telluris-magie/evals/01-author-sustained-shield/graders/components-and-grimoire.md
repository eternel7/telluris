The spell document has a components list containing at least one CONSUMED component (removed from the bag) and at least one CATALYST (carried but not consumed, e.g. consomme:false), and the catalyst's bonus is smaller than the consumed component's bonus. The answer also says the new spell needs a grimoire (a unique grimoire or a scriptorium recipe) before players can learn it.
