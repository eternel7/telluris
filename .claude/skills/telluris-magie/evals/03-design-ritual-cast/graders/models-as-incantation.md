The answer models the multi-turn cast with the casting-time field `incantation`, expressed in action points (PA) and not directly in rounds, and keeps the 12 PM as the launch cost (`cout_pm`). It explains that PM are paid in slices as action points are invested (roughly `cout_pm` divided by `incantation` per PA, or an equivalent statement). It may leave the exact value of `incantation` to the designer or pick one. It does NOT model the multi-turn cast with the per-round upkeep field (`maintien`).
